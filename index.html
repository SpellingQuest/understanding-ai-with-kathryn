Ordering / Service FAQ

What's the AI Jumpstart Call?

A single 60-minute working session — no written plan included. Good for picking one thing to fix and leaving with a clear next step, or for deciding whether Blueprint or Build fits before you commit to either.

What do I get with Blueprint?

A written plan covering your client journey, onboarding boundaries, and a 30-day setup plan — yours to implement on your own timeline.

What do I get with Build?

Everything in Blueprint, plus Kathryn's direct setup work: three scheduled routines built and running, a training walkthrough, a welcome and setup record, and a two-week follow-up to catch anything that needs adjusting.

How many Build clients do you take at once?

One at a time, so each setup gets full attention before the next one starts.

How do I start?

Email understandingaiwithkathryn@gmail.com — there is no self-serve checkout yet. You can preview the intake conversation on the Start the conversation page first; it's a demo, not a live submission.

What are the payment, cancellation, and privacy terms?

Working draft below — not yet finalized by a professional reviewer. Final terms will be shared in writing before you're asked to pay.

Is my data private?

Setup happens in accounts you own and control. Kathryn doesn't ask for or store your passwords or account credentials, and this work does not require sensitive child, school, medical, financial, or credential information. Payment, cancellation & privacy — working draft

This is a draft, not final legal or tax advice. It reflects a reasonable, common-sense starting point and still needs review by a qualified professional before anyone is asked to pay. Nothing here is binding yet.

Payment

AI Jumpstart Call: full payment due at booking. Blueprint and Build: 50% deposit due to hold your date, remaining 50% due at delivery (Blueprint) or at the training session (Build). Payment by invoice; method to be confirmed.

Cancellation & rescheduling

You may reschedule once at no charge with at least 48 hours' notice. Cancelling with less than 48 hours' notice forfeits the deposit. Cancelling more than 7 days before your scheduled session gets a full refund. A no-show is treated as a late cancellation.

Privacy

Kathryn does not collect or store account passwords or login credentials. All setup happens in accounts you own. This work does not require sensitive child, school, medical, financial, or credential information, and session notes use non-sensitive or fictional examples where possible. Your contact information and session notes are kept only as long as needed to deliver the work and are never sold or shared with third parties.

Tax

Applicable sales tax and income reporting depend on jurisdiction and service classification — confirm with a tax professional before this goes live.
